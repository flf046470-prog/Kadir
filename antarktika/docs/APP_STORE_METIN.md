Antarktika, 86° Güney. Araştırma istasyonu HELIKON-14 tam 03:17'de sustu. Buzulun üstünde kalan son mühendis sensin.

## English

**Name:** Antarktika: Whiteout
**Subtitle:** The city beneath the ice
**Promotional text:** 2091. The research team vanished at 03:17. Walk into the whiteout, bring the station back to life and decode the HELIKON signal under the ice.

**Description:** 86° South. Research station HELIKON-14 went silent at exactly 03:17, and you are the last engineer on the glacier. Survive the cold and the storm, restore the station's power, decode a broadcast sent from tomorrow and wake the crystals in a cave that should not exist. A fully offline first-person expedition with real 3D locations, a complete save system, analog touch controls and controller support. No ads, no in-app purchases, no data collection.

**Keywords:** antarctica,exploration,adventure,survival,ice,snow,blizzard,puzzle,mystery,signal,station,first person

## App Review notu (İngilizce)

> The game is fully offline and needs no account. All 3D assets are original (generated in Blender by the developer). The "EKİP" (K) panel lets the player optionally load their own .glb file from Files; nothing is uploaded anywhere. Controls: left half of the screen = movement stick, right half = look, buttons on the right = interact / scan / flashlight / journal.
